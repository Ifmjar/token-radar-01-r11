    The value model — three fits blended, each one tested

    Held-out tests — the blend against the flat multiple

    CV5 is the mean of 4 seeded 5-fold runs; TS1 is the single 2025-01-01 split (train before, test after); TS3 is the mean of 3 time splits (cuts 2024-01-01, 2024-07-01, 2025-01-01). LOO leaves one peer out at a time. The flat multiple is the median multiple of the fit peers, tested the same held-out way. The blend beats it by 0.049 dex on CV5 — and the margins over the plain ridge are inside the seed noise, which this page says out loud below.

    What this radar does not claim

    An implied value is the model's claim about what a token would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is — not a prediction that a token is issued, or when. A protocol that records no token may still have one its DefiLlama record does not carry. Farm steps are estimates: gas moves, and no airdrop is promised. The bands are wide because the model is noisy (CV5 error 0.49 dex): a band that pretended otherwise would be the dishonest one. And the blend's edge over the plain ridge is small — CV5 0.490 vs 0.495, time-split 0.416 vs 0.423 — inside the measurement noise, so this radar is a nominal pass on the numbers, not a proven upgrade.
